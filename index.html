Philosophy

Slow down. Sip intentionally.

Each leaf asks for three to five minutes, and the matcha asks to be whisked. Give it that, and nothing else.

Instruction slip

How to brew

  1. 01

    Measure

    2 g of leaves per 8 oz of water.

  2. 02

    Heat

    175°F for green, jasmine and matcha. 195°F for oolong. 212°F for black and herbal.

  3. 03

    Steep

    3 to 5 minutes. Whisk the matcha instead.

  4. 04

    Pour

    Strain and serve warm.

  5. 05

    Savor

    Breathe in the aroma first.
